## Apify API Pricing
The CLI interacts with the Apify platform ([apify.com](https://apify.com)) to execute the StackOverflow scraper actor:
- **Free Tier / Monthly Credits**: Apify provides $5.00 in free monthly usage credits for personal and evaluation use.
- **Pay As You Go / Subscriptions**: Additional compute units and proxy usage are billed directly by Apify according to actor runtime and data transfer.
- **Custom Actors & Enterprise**: Dedicated proxies and enterprise SLAs are available through Apify.

For current actor pricing and Apify subscription tiers, visit [https://apify.com/pricing](https://apify.com/pricing) or consult the [Apify Console](https://console.apify.com).
